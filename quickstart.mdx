This quickstart takes you from zero to a working app in about ten minutes. You'll create an account, describe what you want to build, watch Clarua generate it, edit it visually, and publish it.

## Prerequisites

- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).
- An email address for your workspace account.
- Optional: a data source (CSV, spreadsheet, or database) if you want to build around your own data.

## Create your first project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [clarua.com](https://clarua.com) and sign up. New accounts land in onboarding, where a live workspace preview assembles as you answer a few questions about what you want to build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want">
    In the chat composer, describe the app in plain language. Be concrete about who it's for and what it does.

    ```text
    Build me a simple CRM for a two-person sales team.
    Contacts have name, company, email, deal stage,
    and last-touched date. I want a pipeline view and
    a contacts list I can filter.
    ```

    If Clarua needs to lock the spec, the [spec interview](/features/build/spec-interview) will ask a short form of clarifying questions before it starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it build">
    The workbench streams the plan, the file changes, and the live preview. Open the [activity side panel](/features/agent/prompt-queue) to see what the agent did on each turn.

    A [build receipt](/features/build/receipts) records files touched, lines changed, time, and cost for every turn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit it in place">
    Click any element in the preview to change text, spacing, color, or layout. Edits are written straight back to source through [visual edit](/features/editor/visual-edit).

    Drop [numbered pins](/features/editor/preview-annotations) on the preview for anything you want the agent to fix on the next pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wire up real data (optional)">
    Open the project's Data panel and connect a [data connector](/features/data/connectors) (Postgres, Redshift, GA, Xero, or others), or paste a CSV directly into chat to [build from a spreadsheet](/features/build/from-spreadsheet).

    Iterate against the **test** environment before promoting to **live**. See [project environments](/features/data/environments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Hit Publish. Clarua runs a should-I-ship check, and if it's green your app deploys to a Clarua-hosted URL. Add [uptime monitoring](/features/security/uptime-monitoring) and a [Trust Center](/features/security/trust-centers) from the project settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
