## Apr 30

**Forge Studio · No-code form builder \+ database connector.** Build a 1–10 question form in the dashboard, link the customer's own database, embed with one script tag. Submissions trigger image generation and write back to tables automatically. AES-256-GCM encrypted service credentials with quarterly rotation.

**Agent Forge · Profession-to-agent generator \+ agentic commerce.** Turn a profession into a launch-ready expert agent with a permanent, PRD-grade system prompt. v2 ships the full agentic commerce suite — forged agents become transactional with payments onboarding, catalog imports, and realtime approval hooks.

**Enterprise · Data-science platform overlay.** Drops the agent layer as a first-class hub service inside the customer's existing data-science cluster. Federated identity through the customer's SSO, env-managed envs, distributed-compute backplane. AES-GCM-encrypted credentials with versioned rotation.
