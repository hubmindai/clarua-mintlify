A running **context meter** shows how full the conversation is, so a long session doesn't hit an invisible wall.

## When it fills up

History is compacted into a **structured summary** that keeps the decisions and drops the noise. Compaction runs:

- **Automatically** at the context limit.
- **On demand** with `/compact` when you want a clean slate mid-session.
- **On session resume** if you're picking up a long conversation from earlier.

## What survives

Compaction preserves:

- **Decisions** you and the agent have settled.
- **Files, endpoints, and identifiers** the conversation depends on.
- **Open threads** that still need action.
- **Constraints and preferences** you stated earlier in the session.

Everything else (chit-chat, tool retries, resolved errors) is dropped or summarized.

## What compaction is not

- **Not memory.** Compaction is per-conversation. Cross-conversation recall comes from [agent memory](/features/agent/memory).
- **Not lossless.** The full transcript stays available; the compacted version is what future turns see. You can always scroll up.
- **Not silent.** Every compaction lands as a message in the transcript, marked as such.

## When to `/compact` on demand

- The conversation just wrapped a large decision and you want the noise gone.
- You're about to change direction (from planning to building, from debug to refactor).
- The context meter is above 70% and you can feel the model losing thread.

## Reading the meter

- **Green.** Comfortable. Ignore it.
- **Amber.** Close to compaction. Consider compacting on your own beat rather than mid-turn.
- **Red.** Auto-compaction is imminent.
