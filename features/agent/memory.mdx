What the agent remembers carries a **live trust score** instead of being taken on faith.

## Aging honestly

- Facts the agent has proven out **decay slowly**.
- Anything reality later contradicts **fades faster** and has to be re-earned.

Stale or disproven knowledge stops getting recalled as if it were still true.

## Tamper-evident

Every sealed fact is written into a tamper-evident chain. A silent edit, deletion, or reordering of history is **structurally detectable**.

## Recall

Only what's still intact, still fresh, and still uncontradicted comes back when the agent reaches for it.

## Related conversations

When you're working on something close to a past conversation, the relevant ones surface on their own, matched by both wording and meaning. Search across your history when you want, and resume any conversation from where it left off.
