Every meaningful step is captured as a snapshot you can return to, so an experiment that goes sideways is one click from undone.

## Cheap to keep

Snapshots are **deduplicated by content**, so they stay cheap to keep and fast to restore. You always have a clean point to fall back to, and you don't pay for it.
