Single-page apps preview the moment you change them.

## No build wait

The view is bundled in the browser, so there's no server round-trip and no build step between an edit and seeing it. The preview is effectively instant, even from a cold start.

## Why it matters

- **Tight feedback loop.** Every visual edit shows immediately, so you iterate on the real thing, not a stale copy.
- **Real behavior.** Preview runs your actual code, not a rendered simulation. State, effects, and interactions all work.
- **Screenshot-worthy at any point.** You can capture what you see and share it, and it will look the same when you open the project later.

## What runs in the browser

- Your components, hydrated with your data.
- Your styles, compiled with your tokens.
- Client-side routing, so every route behaves like it will in production.
- Mock or connected data, depending on your [environment](/features/data/environments).

## What still needs a server

- Server-only rendering paths (SSR, RSC boundaries).
- Real database queries against a [data connector](/features/data/connectors).
- Auth flows that hit a real provider.

For those, Clarua falls back to a server preview on the same URL.

## Per-project toggle

Turn the live preview on or off per project from the project settings. Disable it for projects with heavy backends or when you're doing pure data work.
