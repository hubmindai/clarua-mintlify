Describe what you need to present and get back a complete, structured slide deck: narrative, layout, and content, ready to refine rather than start from a blank page.

## What you get

- A **title slide** and **table of contents**.
- **Sections** with their own openers and transitions.
- **Per-slide layouts** picked to fit the content, not a single template hammered onto everything.
- **Speaker beats** for each slide, so the story flows when you present it.
- **Charts and diagrams** where they help, using [icons, shapes, and charts](/features/build/svg-and-charts).

## Structured, not stitched

The structure is generated so the deck holds together as a narrative instead of reading as a pile of bullet points. Section flow, per-slide layout, and speaker beats are planned before any single slide is written.

## Typical prompt

```text
Make a 12-slide pitch deck for a B2B SaaS product that helps
mid-market ops teams automate onboarding. Audience: skeptical VPs.
Style: confident, data-forward, no jargon. Include a comparison
against Zapier and a slide with our early customer results.
```

## Refining

Once the first draft lands:

- Edit slides in place with the visual editor.
- Regenerate a single slide with a targeted prompt.
- Reorder sections with drag and drop.
- Swap the visual style without losing content.
