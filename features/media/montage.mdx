Montage plans and produces a video from a brief: script, scenes, and assembly handled as a pipeline with **quality gates between stages**, not a single risky one-shot.

## Pipeline stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Brief">
    Describe the video: audience, length, tone, key beats.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Script">
    Montage drafts a script with per-scene notes. Review and edit before it advances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Storyboard">
    Each scene gets a visual plan. Quality gate: is the pacing coherent?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scene generation">
    Veo 3.1 generates each scene. Quality gate: does the output match the storyboard?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assembly">
    Scenes are cut, audio is placed, and the final render lands.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built for real length

Montage is built for explainers and longer-form pieces where the steps need to hold together. Each stage checks its own output before the next one starts, so a bad script never runs through scene generation and rendering. That saves credits and time.

## Veo 3.1

The Montage video pipeline supports Google's **Veo 3.1** video models for scene generation. Higher fidelity, better motion, and better prompt adherence than earlier tiers.

## Working with Montage

- **Iterate on the script** before spending credits on generation.
- **Regenerate a single scene** if one doesn't land, instead of redoing the whole video.
- **Attach reference clips** to guide the style.
- **Export** as MP4 or send straight to a shipping surface with [Maker](/features/media/maker).
