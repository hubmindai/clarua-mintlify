Livey turns a webcam or screen into a **live video AI** feed inside the workbench, powered by Decart.

## Usage

Usage is metered on your workspace credits, so nothing runs unaccounted. Live video sessions show up in your usage breakdown alongside the rest of your work.

## Where it fits

- Live UI feedback on what a user is doing.
- Screenshare-driven debugging.
- Real-time visual QA of the app you're building.
