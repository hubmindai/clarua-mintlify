Livey turns a webcam or screen into a **live video AI** feed inside the workbench, powered by Decart. The agent sees what you see, and reacts as it happens.

## Where it fits

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Live UI feedback" icon="eye">
    Point a camera or screenshare at a real user and get commentary on what's working and what isn't.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Screenshare debugging" icon="bug">
    Share your screen while the agent watches a broken flow and suggests fixes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Visual QA" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    Real-time checks on the app you're building against a set of expected behaviors.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Coaching" icon="user-graduate">
    Use it as a demo review buddy that watches your walkthrough and flags rough edges.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Starting a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a source">
    Webcam, browser tab, application window, or full screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Frame the goal">
    Type what you want the agent to watch for ("tell me when the checkout errors", "spot layout issues on mobile").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stream">
    The feed opens. The agent narrates in the transcript as things happen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    End the session when you're done. The transcript stays with the project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usage and billing

Usage is metered on your workspace credits, so nothing runs unaccounted. Live video sessions show up in your usage breakdown alongside the rest of your work. See [spend limits](/features/workspace/spend-limits) to cap how much a member can use in a month.
