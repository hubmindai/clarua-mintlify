Build a multi-step funnel and test variants against each other, with the math for statistical significance handled for you.

## What you get

- **Clean linking between steps** so a real user flows through it the way you designed.
- **Variant tests** you can read off the numbers instead of guess at.
- A **winning variant** call that's grounded in significance, not vibes.
- Automatic **guardrail metrics** so you know when a win came at another metric's expense.

## Building a funnel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sketch the steps">
    Landing, offer, checkout, thanks. Or whatever your flow looks like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add variants">
    Two or more versions of any step you want to test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Live traffic gets split evenly. You can weight the split if you'd rather ramp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    The winning-variant call arrives once significance is reached. Ship the winner or run another round.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why the math matters

Calling a winner too early is the fastest way to build the wrong thing. Clarua waits for statistical significance before declaring a winner, and shows you:

- The p-value at the moment of the call.
- The expected lift range.
- The sample size behind it.

You know when a result is real and not noise.
