The voice partner carries context across conversations instead of starting cold each time.

## What she remembers

- Your voice **steady vs stressed** and adjusts to it.
- Your **open threads** between sessions, and opens the next call with where things stand.
- Each speaker's facts **keyed to their own voice** in a group call.

## Group awareness

In a group call, the voice partner reads the room: making space for a quiet voice, easing off when one person dominates.

## Follow-through

Every call now lands on one concrete decision or next step, and she checks in on it next time. A good conversation turns into something that actually moves.
