The voice partner carries context across conversations instead of starting cold each time. She remembers where you left off, reads the room in a group call, and follows through on the next step.

## What she remembers

- Your voice **steady vs stressed** and adjusts to it.
- Your **open threads** between sessions, and opens the next call with where things stand.
- Each speaker's facts **keyed to their own voice** in a group call.
- Preferences you've stated ("keep it short", "explain the reasoning").

Memory is trust-scored and tamper-evident the same way as [agent memory](/features/agent/memory).

## Group awareness

In a group call, the voice partner reads the room: making space for a quiet voice, easing off when one person dominates. She can:

- Address someone by name once she's heard it in the call.
- Distinguish speakers by voice, so notes attribute correctly.
- Summarize on demand ("recap what we've decided so far").

## Follow-through

Every call now lands on one concrete decision or next step, and she checks in on it next time. A good conversation turns into something that actually moves.

- The decision or next step is saved to the project.
- The next call opens by naming it.
- If you resolved it between calls, she notices and moves on.
