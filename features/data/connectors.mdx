Data connectors bring real data directly into the projects you build.

## Included connectors

- Amazon Redshift.
- Microsoft Fabric.
- Google Analytics.
- Xero.
- Plus the existing catalog of database, SaaS, and file sources.

## Dedicated connection pages

Health and Finances each have their own connection pages, with a blank-slate design tuned for the real thing you're about to wire up.

## Test and live environments

Every project has separate **test** and **live** environments, so you can iterate against one and publish from the other without conflict. Environments never collide on add.
