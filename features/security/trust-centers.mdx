Any app you publish can generate a **Trust Center**: a public page that surfaces its security posture from what's already known about it.

## No extra setup

The Trust Center is generated from your existing signals: security scans, firewall verdicts, uptime data, audit exports. You don't fill out a form.

## What it shows

- Current security grade for the app.
- Uptime and reliability posture.
- Data handling and connector inventory.

## Should-I-ship verdict

When you're ready to deploy, Clarua answers the whole question in one move. She re-checks everything you'd marked as proven against its actual source, flags evidence that has gone stale, and pools every check into a single calibrated verdict: **ship**, **review**, or **hold**. Checks that have proven reliable weigh more than ones that haven't.
