See your whole workspace at a glance: a single **A to F security grade** rolled up from every project, blending each app's code-security score with its supply-chain firewall score.

## What it surfaces

- Apps blocked from publishing.
- Total open **critical** and **high-severity** issues.
- Your worst offenders ranked first.
- Projects that have **never been scanned at all**, because an unknown is a risk, not a pass.

## Rollup, not average

The grade is a rollup, not an average that hides bad projects behind good ones. One project failing hard shows up clearly at the workspace level.
