Forward your audit log to the security tooling your team already runs.

## Formats

- Newline-delimited JSON.
- CEF.
- Plain JSON.

## Delivery

- **Pull.** Your collector fetches batches on its own cadence, with a cursor that never double-counts or skips.
- **Push.** New events go to a webhook in near real time, each delivery signed so the receiver can prove it came from you.

## Safety and privacy

- Sensitive fields can be redacted on the way out.
- Deliveries retry on transient failures and stop cleanly on permanent ones.
- Only a **public endpoint** is a valid destination, so the export can never be pointed inward.
