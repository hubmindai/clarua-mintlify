The billing dashboard is production-ready.

## What you can do

- Manage subscription and payment method directly from Billing.
- Buy usage credits from Settings without leaving the app.
- See your usage breakdown as percentages instead of raw numbers.
- Hand off to Stripe reliably when you need the full billing portal.

## Invites on paid plans

Paid workspaces can invite as many teammates by email as they need. The previous per-plan cap is gone.
