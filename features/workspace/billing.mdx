The billing dashboard is production-ready. Everything lives in the workspace, with a clean hand-off to Stripe when you need the full portal.

## What you can do

- **Manage subscription and payment method** directly from Billing.
- **Buy usage credits** from Settings without leaving the app.
- **See your usage breakdown as percentages** instead of raw numbers.
- **Download invoices** and receipts.
- **Change seat count** for team plans.
- **Hand off to Stripe** reliably when you need the full billing portal.

## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Trying Clarua | Metered daily budget |
| Pro | Individual builders | Full agent, higher limits |
| Team | Small teams | Shared workspace, roles, [spend limits](/features/workspace/spend-limits) |
| Enterprise | Larger orgs | SSO, [managed registry](/features/workspace/managed-registry), audit export, dedicated support |

## Invites on paid plans

Paid workspaces can invite as many teammates by email as they need. The previous per-plan cap is gone.

## Credits and usage

- Credits are metered per member. See [spend limits](/features/workspace/spend-limits) for how to control them.
- Percentages in the UI make usage legible ("62% used" beats "\$147.13 of \$200").
- Buying credits mid-cycle takes seconds and applies immediately.
