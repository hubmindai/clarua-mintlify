The referrer grant is **100 credits**, and the reward pays out when the friend subscribes to Pro or above.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grab your referral link">
    From the sidebar footer, share modal, or user menu. Every entry points to the same personal link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share">
    Send it to a friend, colleague, or community. Attribution kicks in the moment they open the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They subscribe">
    When your referral upgrades to Pro or above, the reward posts to your account automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redeem">
    Credits show up in [billing](/features/workspace/billing) and can be used like any other credit balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Consistent surfaces

Every referral surface follows the same number and links to the same affiliate flow:

- Sidebar footer.
- Share modal.
- User menu.

## Tracking

The affiliate tracker installs on every page, and conversions attribute correctly. Your dashboard shows:

- Total referrals sent.
- How many converted, and when.
- Pending versus paid credit.
- Any active bonus program.
