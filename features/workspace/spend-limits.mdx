---
title: "Spend limits"
description: "A monthly budget per workspace member, with per-person overrides and pre-flight enforcement."
icon: "wallet"
---

Admins can set a monthly spending cap per workspace and override it for any individual.

## Per person, per month

- Raise a power user.
- Cap a contractor.
- Freeze a member at zero.

Spend is metered per person in exact cents and resets cleanly at the start of each month.

## Enforced before the fact

- A **warning** the moment someone approaches their limit.
- A **pre-flight check** that holds back work which would push them over.

Budgets are enforced before the spend happens, not reconciled after.

## Buying credits

Members can purchase additional usage credits from Settings without leaving the app, and see their usage breakdown as percentages instead of raw numbers.
