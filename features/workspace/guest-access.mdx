Invite someone outside your workspace as a **guest** and give them access to exactly the apps you choose, nothing else, through a share link. No account or single sign-on to set up.

## When to use guest links

- Show a stakeholder the current build without adding them to the workspace.
- Give an external agency access to a specific app during an engagement.
- Send a live preview to a customer for feedback via [preview annotations](/features/editor/preview-annotations).
- Provide a portal to a shipped app for a small pilot audience.

## Scope per link

Each guest gets a **high-entropy link** you can:

- **Scope** to one app or many.
- **Set to expire** at a specific time or after a set number of hours.
- **Restrict to view or comment**, not edit.
- **Revoke** the moment you want.

## Admin table

One admin table shows:

- Who has access.
- Which apps they can reach.
- When they were last active.
- When the link expires.
- Who created the link and when.

Filter, sort, and revoke in bulk from the same view.

## Link is the credential

The link is the credential: shown once, checked in constant time on every request, and stops working the instant you revoke it or it expires. Practically that means:

- **Save it in a password manager**, not in an email thread.
- **Rotate on any suspicion.** Revoke the old, generate a new one; the guest keeps their access without you re-adding anything.
- **Don't share a link scoped to more than the guest needs.** Create per-app links instead of a workspace-wide one.
