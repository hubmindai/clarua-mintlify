Every build turn records exactly what happened.

## What's captured

- **Files touched** on the turn, with per-file line-add and line-remove counts.
- **Total lines** added and removed, from real before-and-after file comparisons.
- **Time taken** end to end.
- **Cost** for the turn, split by model calls, tool use, and background compute.
- **Turn outcome**: success, partial, or reverted.

## Persistent

The summary is saved with the message. Refresh the page, come back tomorrow, or open any past turn and you get the same receipt. Receipts are searchable across a project's history.

## Not an estimate

Because line counts come from real diffs and cost comes from the actual usage, receipts are the ground truth. They aren't projections and they don't drift.

## Reading a receipt

- **Green header.** The turn completed. Everything applied.
- **Amber header.** Partial: some files applied, some deferred (usually behind a plan-mode gate).
- **Grey header.** Reverted through a [checkpoint](/features/editor/checkpoints) or an undo.

Expand any receipt to see the per-file breakdown. Click a file to open its diff.

## Aggregating receipts

Receipts roll up into:

- **Per-project usage** in the project settings.
- **Per-member spend**, which powers [spend limits](/features/workspace/spend-limits).
- **Workspace analytics** for admins.
