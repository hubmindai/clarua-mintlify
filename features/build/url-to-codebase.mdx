Give Clarua a URL and the Build agent produces a codebase that mirrors the page's structure. Layout, sections, copy, and asset placeholders come across as source, not a static clone.

## Good for

- Rebuilding a **landing page you own** in your stack.
- Kicking off a **redesign** from a reference implementation.
- Extracting the **structure of a component-heavy page** to use as a starting point.
- Porting a page from one framework to another with the structure preserved.

## Please don't use it to

- Clone a site you don't own or have permission to reproduce.
- Bypass a paywall or scrape content behind auth. Clarua respects `robots.txt` and refuses obvious cases.

## What Clarua extracts

- **Layout tree.** Sections, containers, and their arrangement.
- **Typography and color hints.** Pulled into token candidates you can accept or replace.
- **Copy.** Placed inline as editable strings.
- **Asset placeholders.** Images and icons noted but not embedded (you supply your own).
- **Routing hints.** Multi-page sites become multi-route scaffolds.

## Not a scraper

The output is a project scaffold, not a snapshot. You get editable components, real routing, and a clean starting point rather than a locked HTML dump. Nothing is loaded from the source site at runtime.

## Typical flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    Any public URL. For multiple pages, paste them one per turn or as a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the scope">
    Clarua shows what it extracted and which sections it will build. Trim or expand before it writes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace tokens and assets">
    Point Clarua at your token library and asset library so hex values and stock images become yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate">
    Use [visual edit](/features/editor/visual-edit) and [preview annotations](/features/editor/preview-annotations) to polish, exactly like any other build.
  </Step>
</Steps>
